Activities

Hiking

I'm an avid hiker and enjoy exploring the outdoors. Some of my favorite hikes include:

Angel's Landing

I hiked the Angels Landing hike in Zion National Park in Utah during my freshman year of high school. It's known as the most dangerous hike in United States, and the steep drop-offs were definitely vertigo-inducing when I was hiking it.

Skyline Hike

While the Skyline Hike wasn't the most dangerous hike I've done, it was one of the most breathtaking. Mount Rainier, the national park that the hike is located in, is my favorite for its beauty, and the Skyline Hike is an amazing summary of this beauty.

Wheeler Peak

Wheeler Peak is the highest peak in New Mexico, and also the first mountain I ever summited. I hiked it with my dad and childhood friend when I was 12 years old, and it was a really tough hike! However, I credit it for being the catalyst for my love of more strenuous hikes.

Romsdalseggen Ridge

The Romsdalseggen Ridge hike is the most strenuous, most dangerous, and most beautiful hike I've ever done. Going to Norway was a dream of mine for a few years, and I was lucky enough to be able to go there with my family before I started college. The hike was extreme, with a lot of steep drop-offs (and only a few chains to hold onto) and exhausting scrambles, but the views were the prettiest I've ever seen.

Volunteer Tutoring

In my free time over the summer, I volunteer to tutor immigrant children in English and Math, focusing on preparing them for the STAAR test administered in Texas. I primarily do one-on-one or small group tutoring, and it's always an interesting experience adapting to the different learning styles and language barriers of the students I tutor.
